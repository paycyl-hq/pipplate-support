🚨 Urgent Medical & Anaphylaxis Emergency Protocol

PipPlate is a parent-recorded food journal and informational organization tool — NOT a medical device. If your infant or child exhibits any signs of an acute allergic reaction or anaphylaxis — including difficulty breathing, wheezing, swelling of the face, lips, tongue, or throat, generalized hives, repetitive vomiting, sudden diarrhea, or severe lethargy/floppiness — STOP using this application and call your local emergency medical services (e.g., 911 in the US/Canada, 112 in Europe/Türkiye, 999 in the UK, 119 in Japan) immediately.

Use of the App does not create a physician-patient or dietitian-client relationship.

1.2 Mandatory Pediatrician Consultation

Every infant develops at a different pace and has unique nutritional and medical needs (including prematurity, eczema, reflux, dysphagia, or existing food allergies). You must consult a licensed pediatrician, pediatric allergist, or registered pediatric dietitian before:

  • Starting complementary solid foods or Baby-Led Weaning (BLW);
  • Introducing any of the major food allergens (cow's milk, egg, peanut, tree nuts, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish, sesame) or any other novel food;
  • Re-introducing a food after any suspected adverse reaction; or
  • Modifying your infant's diet based on cultural/dietary filters (Standard, Halal, Kosher, Vegetarian).

1.3 Infant Botulism Warning (Strict Prohibition of Honey Under 12 Months)

NEVER feed honey (raw, pasteurized, cooked, or baked into products) to an infant under 12 months of age. Honey can contain spores of Clostridium botulinum, which causes infant botulism—a rare but potentially fatal paralytic illness. Even if honey or a honey-containing food appears in a custom log or AI scan result, parents must strictly exclude it for babies under 1 year old.

1.4 Choking Prevention, Supervision & Texture Responsibility

Gagging and choking are serious risks during solid food introduction. While PipPlate displays general age-grouped preparation notes (e.g., 6–9 months palmar grasp soft spears vs. 9–12 months pincer grasp bite-sized pieces), the parent or caregiver physically preparing and serving the meal bears 100% sole responsibility for verifying food temperature, softness, cut geometry, and safety:

  • Always test food softness between your thumb and index finger before serving.
  • Never leave an infant or toddler unattended while eating. Ensure the child is sitting fully upright in a secure highchair.
  • Never serve high-risk choking hazards whole, including whole nuts or seeds, thick globs of nut butter, whole grapes, cherry tomatoes, raw apple/carrot chunks, popcorn, hard crackers, marshmallows, or coin-sliced hot dogs/sausages.

1.5 Configurable Allergen Observation Window & Pediatrician PDF Reports

  • Observation Window Timer: PipPlate includes a user-configurable observation counter (adjustable between 2 and 5 days; default 3 days) between new allergen introductions. This timer is a user-controlled reminder tool, not a clinical protocol or guarantee of allergy safety. Allergic reactions can occur on the first exposure or after many previously tolerated exposures.
  • Pediatrician Report (PDF): The PDF export feature generates a formatted summary of parent-recorded logs only. It is not a certified medical record or "doctor-approved" clinical diagnosis. We make no representations regarding the accuracy or completeness of data entered by the user.

🤖 2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Plate Scanner Disclaimer & Hidden Allergen Warning

2.1 Automated Visual Estimation Only

The "Snap-a-Plate" AI scanning feature utilizes computer vision models to suggest possible visible ingredients from a photograph of a plate. AI vision outputs are probabilistic estimates and frequently make errors, omissions, or misclassifications.

2.2 Inability to Detect Hidden Allergens or Cross-Contamination

CRITICAL ALLERGY WARNING: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE CANNOT SEE OR DETECT HIDDEN INGREDIENTS, BAKED-IN ALLERGENS, SAUCES, BROTHS, SPICES, COOKING OILS, DAIRY/BUTTER, EGG WASHES, SOY LECITHIN, WHEAT/GLUTEN, SESAME PASTE (TAHINI), PEANUT/TREE NUT TRACES, OR KITCHEN CROSS-CONTAMINATION. NEVER RELY ON PIPPLATE'S AI SCANNER TO DETERMINE WHETHER A FOOD IS SAFE FOR AN ALLERGIC INFANT.

Every AI scan result requires explicit parent review and manual confirmation before saving to your journal. You agree that you will independently verify all food labels, recipes, and preparation methods before feeding your child or logging an exposure.

🔐 3. Privacy Policy (COPPA, GDPR Art. 9, CCPA & Health Data Compliance)

3.1 Core Privacy Principle: 100% Local-First Architecture

PipPlate was engineered from the ground up to protect infant and family privacy. We do not require user accounts, email registration, or cloud profiles. All personal and health-related records you create in PipPlate—including your baby's name, birth date, solids start date, daily food tasting logs, acceptance ratings, symptom/reaction logs, and attached reaction photos—are stored exclusively on your local device inside an on-device SQLite database and sandboxed application file storage.

3.2 Categories of Data & How They Are Processed

Data Category Storage Location Purpose & Retention
Baby Profile & Health Logs (Name, DOB, Food Tastings, Allergen Exposures, Symptoms, Reaction Photos) 100% Local Device Only (On-device SQLite & local sandbox) Used solely to render your in-app journal, milestone cards, and local PDF reports. Never uploaded to our servers. Retained on your device until you delete the app or tap "Delete All My Data".
Snap-a-Plate Camera Photos (Optional AI Food Scan) Transient / Ephemeral Processing Only When you actively tap the shutter in Snap-a-Plate, the compressed food image is sent via encrypted HTTPS through our Cloudflare Worker proxy to the Google Gemini Vision API solely to return ingredient labels. Images are processed in memory and immediately discarded; they are never stored on our servers, never linked to your identity, and never used for facial recognition.
Subscription & Entitlement Status (PipPlate Plus) Apple App Store / RevenueCat (Anonymous App User ID) Processed via Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat solely to verify active PipPlate Plus entitlements and restore purchases across devices. No child health data is ever shared with RevenueCat.
Crash & Diagnostic Logs Sentry (Anonymized Technical Telemetry) Crash stack traces, OS version, and device model used strictly to maintain ≥99.8% crash-free stability. Personal identifiers, baby names, and food/reaction logs are stripped before transmission.

3.3 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

PipPlate is a tool designed exclusively for adult parents and legal guardians (aged 18+) to log their own observations. The App is not directed at children under 13, and children do not interact with the App. Because all child profile information (first name/nickname, birth date, feeding logs, and photos) remains strictly local on the parent's device and is never transmitted to or stored on any remote server operated by us, we do not collect, store, sell, or share Personal Information of children under 13 within the meaning of COPPA (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506).

3.4 GDPR Article 9 (EU/UK), KVKK (Türkiye) & Washington MHMDA Compliance

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Article 9), Turkish Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK No. 6698), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), data concerning allergic reactions and infant nutrition may constitute Consumer Health Data or Special Category Data:

  • Data Controller of Local Logs: Because your logs reside solely on your device and we have zero technical access to read, view, or recover them, you act as the sole custodian of your local database.
  • Legal Basis for Processing: Explicit user consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)) obtained during onboarding and upon triggering camera permissions, and performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) for verifying subscription entitlements.
  • Zero Sale, Zero Ad-Tracking, Zero Profiling: We never sell, rent, or monetize user data or health records. PipPlate contains zero third-party behavioral advertising trackers (no IDFA tracking).

3.5 Device Permissions

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Requested only when you open Snap-a-Plate or take a reaction photo. Never records video or audio.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription / NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Used only when you manually attach an existing photo to a log or save a Milestone Share Card / PDF report to your phone.
  • Local Notifications: Scheduled locally on your device for optional allergen observation window check-ins and backup reminders (respecting 21:00–08:00 quiet hours).

3.6 Backups, Export & Right to Erasure ("Delete All My Data")

You can exercise your right to data portability and erasure immediately inside the App without contacting us:

  • Portability: Go to Settings → Backup & Restore → Export Backup to export a complete .pipplate JSON/ZIP archive of your local database.
  • Instant Erasure: Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete All My Data to permanently and irreversibly wipe all baby profiles, food logs, reaction records, and settings from your device, or simply uninstall the App.
