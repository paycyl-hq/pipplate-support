⚖️ Mandatory Disclaimers, Feeding Safety & Legal Notices

🩺 1. Non-Medical Device Disclaimer (Apple Guideline 1.4.1 Compliance)

PipPlate is an informational food diary and parent-tracking log designed solely to help caregivers organize meal notes. PipPlate is not a medical device, clinical decision support system, or diagnostic tool, and has not been evaluated by the FDA, EMA, or any regulatory health authority. All "Pediatrician Reports" generated by the app are strictly parent-recorded summaries and do not constitute official clinical records or medical advice. Always consult a qualified pediatrician, pediatric dietitian, or board-certified allergist before starting solids, introducing allergenic foods, or making dietary changes.

🍼 2. Infant Feeding Safety, Choking Prevention & Botulism Warning

  • Infant Botulism Warning (No Honey Under 12 Months): Never serve honey or foods containing raw/cooked honey to infants under 12 months of age due to the risk of infant botulism.
  • Choking Hazard & Supervision: Always supervise your infant closely while eating. Ensure baby is seated upright in a highchair. Never serve whole nuts, whole grapes, cherry tomatoes, raw hard vegetables/fruits, popcorn, or coin-shaped hot dogs. Always prepare foods in age-appropriate softness and cut shapes (e.g., squish-testable spears for palmar grasp or bite-sized pieces for pincer grasp) as advised by your pediatrician.
  • Configurable Observation Window: The allergen observation counter in PipPlate is a user-configurable tracking timer (2–5 days) and does not assert a mandatory clinical protocol. Follow your healthcare provider's specific allergen introduction schedule.

🤖 3. AI Plate Scanner Limitation & Hidden Allergen Warning

The Snap-a-Plate AI feature provides automated visual estimates of visible foods only. AI cannot detect hidden ingredients, cross-contamination, cooking oils, spices, dairy, egg, soy, wheat, or nut traces mixed inside cooked dishes, baked goods, or sauces. Parents and caregivers must manually inspect, verify, and confirm all ingredients before serving food or saving a log. When you trigger a scan, the captured plate image is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS solely for immediate visual ingredient detection and is never stored on our servers, used to train AI models, or used for facial recognition.

🔐 4. Child Privacy, COPPA, GDPR Art. 9 & Health Data Protection

PipPlate is built on a 100% Local-First Architecture in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR Article 9 (Special Categories of Personal Data), CCPA, and the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA):

  • Zero Cloud Storage of Child Data: Your baby's name, birth date, tasting logs, symptom notes, and reaction photos are stored exclusively on your personal device inside a local SQLite database. We have no user accounts and no cloud database of your child's health records.
  • Data Ownership & Deletion: You retain 100% ownership of your data. You can export a full local backup (.pipplate JSON/ZIP) at any time or permanently erase all records from your device in one tap via Settings → Danger Zone → Delete All My Data.
